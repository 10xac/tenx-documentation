# FAQ

Quick answers to the questions that come up most often. Each answer links to
the full guide section where the workflow is documented.

> Don't see your question here? See [Glossary](glossary.md) for terminology, or contact `support@10academy.org`.

---

## Account & access

??? question "1. I don't have a login link"
    Applicants receive the link from their program announcement. Staff are
    provisioned by the program team and receive credentials by email. If neither
    applies, contact your platform administrator or `support@10academy.org`.

    → See [Apply — Accessing the Platform](platforms/apply/index.md#14-accessing-the-platform) and [Tenx Learning — How to Access](platforms/tenx-learning/index.md#14-how-to-access-the-platform).

??? question "2. I forgot my password"
    Click **Forgot password** on the login screen, enter your registered email,
    then follow the link in the reset email. If the email doesn't arrive within a
    few minutes, check spam before requesting another link.

    → Full steps for [Apply](platforms/apply/index.md#22-password-management) · [Tenx Learning](platforms/tenx-learning/index.md#22-password-management).

??? question "3. I can sign in to one platform but not another"
    Apply, Tenx Learning, and Leap each have their own access provisioning. Being
    in one cohort doesn't automatically grant access to all three. Contact your
    program team to confirm which platforms are enabled for your account.

## For trainees / applicants

??? question "4. My submission window is closed — can I still submit?"
    No. Once the closing date passes, the portal no longer accepts submissions.
    You'll see a red "Submission Window Closed" banner. Contact your program
    manager or tutor for any exception.

    → [Submission Window Closed view](platforms/tenx-learning/trainee.md#4353-assignment-detail-submission-window-closed).

??? question "5. How is my rubric score calculated?"
    Each criterion (Problem Definition, Analysis, etc.) is scored independently
    and shown as a progress bar with the achieved/maximum score. The total mark is
    the sum across criteria.

    → [Rubric Scoring](platforms/tenx-learning/trainee.md#4354-assignment-detail-submitted-on-time).

??? question "6. What happens if I refresh during a timed assessment?"
    Don't. Refreshing or closing the tab during an active assessment may end your
    attempt or lose answers. Wait for the confirmation screen before navigating
    away.

    → [Taking an Assessment](platforms/apply/applicant.md#45-taking-an-assessment).

??? question "7. How do I import my profile from LinkedIn or a CV?"
    In Tenx Learning, click **Update Profile → LinkedIn Profile URL** and paste a
    public LinkedIn URL, or use the **Upload CV** tab to drop a file. The platform
    extracts and populates fields automatically.

    → [Generate Profile from LinkedIn or CV](platforms/tenx-learning/trainee.md#454-generate-profile-from-linkedin-or-cv).

## For instructors / staff

??? question "8. How do I bulk-email applicants without revealing their addresses?"
    Always use the **BCC** field. The platform pre-populates BCC with the selected
    applicants by default precisely so addresses stay private. Don't move them to
    To or CC.

    → [Bulk Email Communication](platforms/apply/staff.md#bulk-email-communication).

??? question "9. How do I regrade a submission?"
    Open the grading screen, click **Regrade**, optionally enter tutor feedback
    explaining why, then click **Regrade** to trigger a new evaluation. The
    previous grade is preserved in Grade History.

    → [Regrade Assignment](platforms/tenx-learning/staff.md#3288-regrade-assignment-re-evaluation-workflow).

??? question "10. A trainee can't see a module I assigned — what's wrong?"
    Two checks: (1) is the module's **Topic** assigned to that trainee, and
    (2) was the trainee selected in the module's **Trainees Step** during creation.
    Both have to be true.

    → [Assign Trainees to Topic](platforms/tenx-learning/staff.md#assign-trainees-to-topic) · [Trainees Step](platforms/tenx-learning/staff.md#trainees-step-assign-module-to-trainees).

??? question "11. Should I use Smart, AI, or Manual rubrics?"
    - **Smart Rubrics** are AI-assisted. Best for quick setup and standardised evaluation.
    - **Rubrics with AI** auto-generates from criteria. Best for scaled assessment.
    - **Manual Rubrics** are staff-defined. Use when you need full control over scoring.

    → [Create Rubric Options](platforms/tenx-learning/staff.md#3272-create-rubric-options).

??? question "12. Only Qualified questions in live assessments — why?"
    Questions in the bank carry a status tag (Raw, Qualified, Unqualified, Mock).
    Only **Qualified** questions have been reviewed and approved for live use. The
    others are in-progress or rejected. Use the Question Bank Overview to confirm
    qualified-question coverage before publishing.

    → [Question Bank Overview](platforms/apply/staff.md#3333-overview).

??? question "13. Why is the Reviews card empty?"
    It only populates after reviewers start evaluating applications. If the card
    shows "No data available", reviewers haven't begun yet — check that you've
    created a review configuration and assigned reviewers.

    → [Create Review](platforms/apply/staff.md#3441-create-review).

## Platform questions

??? question "14. When do interviews happen — Apply or Leap?"
    **Leap.** Apply runs **quizzes / pre-assessments** (timed, automatically
    scored). Interviews are part of the [Leap](platforms/leap/index.md) hiring
    flow, not Apply. The *Interview* form type in Apply's Form Builder is just the
    template that Leap uses for capturing interviewer responses.

??? question "15. What's the difference between a Workspace and a Cohort?"
    A **Workspace** is a program (e.g., AI Engineering, Project Management). A
    **Cohort** is a specific intake within that workspace (e.g., Batch 4). One
    workspace can contain many cohorts.

    → [Workspace Selection](platforms/apply/staff.md#31-workspace-selection).

??? question "16. How does data flow between Apply, Tenx Learning, and Leap?"
    Apply selects applicants → accepted applicants become trainees on Tenx
    Learning → trainees who complete training move into Leap for career
    readiness. Profile data carries forward through each stage.

    → [Platform Overview](platform-overview.md).

??? question "17. What's *iPersona* / *Parrot*?"
    Two names for the same Leap app. **Parrot** is the internal name; **iPersona**
    is the brand name in the UI. It's the AI service that analyses CVs and
    extracts competencies.

    → [Apps — Parrot (iPersona)](apps.md#parrot-ipersona-cv-analysis-enhancement).

??? question "18. What's *Frog*, *Eagle*, *CSS*?"
    All Leap apps. **Frog** = job recommendation engine. **Eagle** = engagement
    monitoring and notifications. **CSS** = Career Scenario Simulator (interactive
    coaching).

    → [Apps — Leap Apps](apps.md#leap-apps).
