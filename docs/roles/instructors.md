# Instructors

> **Terminology** — *Instructors* covers everyone who delivers training and
> evaluates work on Tenx: **Program Coordinators**, **Reviewers**, and
> **Tutors**. The in-product UI uses **Staff** as the umbrella term. Treat
> *instructor* and *staff* as interchangeable throughout this documentation.

Instructors are responsible for designing learning experiences, evaluating
trainee work, and supporting cohorts as they progress through the program.
This page is a **complete map** of what an instructor can do across the Tenx
platforms, with each item linking to the step-by-step guide where it is
documented.

## 7. Need help?

- **Forgot password?** Reset from the [Apply login](../platforms/apply/index.md#22-password-management) or [Tenx Learning login](../platforms/tenx-learning/index.md#22-password-management) page
- **Locked out / no platform link?** Contact `support@10academy.org`
- **Trainee can't access content?** Check that the topic and module are assigned to them — see [Assign Trainees to Topic](../platforms/tenx-learning/staff.md#assign-trainees-to-topic) and [Trainees Step](../platforms/tenx-learning/staff.md#trainees-step-assign-module)
- **Bulk email not sending?** Verify recipients are in BCC (auto-populated) and that the subject is set — see [Bulk Email Communication](../platforms/apply/staff.md#bulk-email-communication)
