Trainee Quick Guide

Tenx Application Portal

Tenx Application Portal Applicant Side Features

User Registration

Trainees begin their journey with Tenx by registering and creating an account, a process designed to be user-friendly and accessible.

Snapshot: Registration page

Form Submission

Trainees are required to complete initial assessment forms that capture essential details about their educational qualifications, work experiences, and professional aspirations. This process, similar to using a Google Form, ensures that all pertinent information is systematically gathered to support their training and development.

Snapshot; The Form interface

Personalized Quizzes

Tenx offers a personalized quiz interface by providing tailored questions based on each applicant's skill level.

Snapshot: Quiz interface

Cohort Selection and Management

After completing the initial setup, trainees can select and manage their involvement in multiple cohorts or projects, allowing them to diversify their learning experiences and engage with various subjects and peer groups.

Snapshot: Cohort selection interface

Onboarding

The onboarding process is designed to engage trainees from the start. It includes introductory information about the Tenx platform.

Interactive Challenges

Tenx provides an engaging challenges interface designed to enhance learning through practical exercises.

Assignment Submissions

Trainees can submit and edit their assignments, which are central to their learning process. This feature supports interactive and self-paced learning.

Trainees receive personalized feedback on their submissions and can manage this feedback to improve their work and skills.
