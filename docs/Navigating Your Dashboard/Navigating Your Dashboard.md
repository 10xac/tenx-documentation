# Navigating Your Dashboard

Your Tenx dashboard is the command-centre for everything you do on the platform. It surfaces your progress, deadlines, feedback and career signals in one place so you can act quickly and stay on track.

### **1\. Home Header**

| Area | What you’ll see | Action you can take |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Program Switcher** | Current program / cohort name | Jump between multiple cohorts if you’re enrolled in more than one. |
| **Notification Bell** | Real-time alerts on new feedback, deadlines and job-match updates | Click to open the notification tray and mark items as read. |
| **Profile Menu** | Avatar \+ quick links (Profile, Settings, Help) | Update settings or log out. |

### **2\. Performance Snapshot**

A compact banner that updates every Monday at 00:00 EAT:

* **Weekly Score** – composite of quiz, assignment and engagement points.

* **Leaderboard Rank** – where you stand against peers this week.

* **Streak Counter** – consecutive weeks with all core tasks submitted.

### **3\. Task Tiles**

Each tile shows:

1. **Status dot**

   * Green \= completed

   * Amber \= in progress

   * Red \= pending/overdue

2. **Task name** (e.g., “Challenge 5: SQL Joins”)

3. **Due date & time**

4. **Quick actions** – *View brief*, *Submit work*, *Check feedback*

Hover-states reveal percentage completeness so you can prioritise.

### **4\. Progress Charts**

Click **View Details** on the Performance Snapshot to open full analytics:

* **Radar Chart** – competency growth across SFIA domains.

* **Bar Graph** – quiz vs assignment scores over time.

* **Heat Map** – daily activity (submissions, comments, reactions).

* **Download CSV** – export raw data for personal tracking.

All charts update in real time as soon as a quiz/assignment is graded.
