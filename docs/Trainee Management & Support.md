# **Tenx Trainee Management & Support Manual**

## **1\. Purpose**

Equip **Staff (Admins)** and **Super Admins** with clear procedures for onboarding, engaging, supporting, and tracking trainees throughout the Tenx.

---

## **2\. Key Stakeholders & Responsibilities**

| Stakeholder | Core Responsibilities |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Super Admin** | • Configure global trainee‑support policies • Enable roadmap features (automated follow‑ups, iPersona integration) |
| **Staff – Trainee Support** | • Monitor submissions, engagement, satisfaction, and experience • Send bulk emails & notifications • Manage issues, escalations, and data‑deletion requests • Oversee job‑placement assistance and Eagle/Nana dashboards |
| **Trainee** | • Complete forms, quizzes, and submissions • Interact with Nana for learning and support • Review grades, leaderboards, dashboards • Manage job applications and personal data |

---

## **3\. Trainee Lifecycle & Staff Actions**

### **3.1 Application & Onboarding**

1. **Public Registration & Form Intake** – verify form completeness, CV insights.

2. **Assign Cohort / Program** – place accepted applicants into appropriate cohort.

3. **Static Onboarding Docs** – publish program details.

4. **(Planned) Dynamic System Tour** – enable first‑time user walkthrough and chatbot context selection.

### **3.2 Learning Phase**

* **Submission Management** – track assignments, grades, and peer reviews.

* **Performance Dashboards** – weekly best submissions, engagement metrics, competency radar.

* **Nana Support** – context‑aware Q\&A, dynamic charts, personalised recommendations.

* **Notifications & Reminders** – schedule via bulk email and in‑app alerts.

### **3.3 Monitoring & Feedback**

Current: manual follow‑ups, survey distribution, issue logging.  
 Planned: automated engagement/satisfaction tracking, complaint workflow, weekly Eagle alerts.

### **3.4 Job Placement**

* **Leap Tools** – manage profiles, competencies, evidence, application status.

* **Staff Oversight** – review job reactions, assets, and SJS insights; update job statuses.

* **Roadmap** – LinkedIn API applications on behalf of trainees, iPersona insights, job simulations, search & share options.

---

## **4\. Support Channels & Tools**

| Tool | Staff Usage | Trainee Experience |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Nana** | Configure knowledge base, communication settings, schedule updates | Chatbot with context switching, dynamic charts & dashboards |
| **Eagle** | Monitor engagement, satisfaction, experience; receive alerts | *N/A* (internal analytics) |
| **Forms & Surveys** | Create feedback forms, evaluate responses | Submit complaints, feedback, satisfaction surveys |
| **Notifications** | Bulk email, in‑app alerts, reminders | Receive per preset preferences |
| **Issue Tracking** | Log, escalate, resolve user/system issues | Report issues; track resolution status |

---

## **5\. Engagement & Satisfaction Strategy (2024‑2025)**

* **Automated Follow‑Ups** – trigger based on low engagement or performance.

* **Satisfaction Metrics** – use Eagle to visualise trends and act on declines.

* **Wellbeing Monitoring** – Nana Community‑Manager mode to assess cohort morale.

* **Personalised Dashboards** – trainees review weekly progress to foster self‑reflection.

---

## **6\. Data Privacy & Requests**

| Process | Staff Action |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Data Download** | Export trainee tables for authorised analysis. |
| **Deletion / Archiving** | Approve trainee requests; archive or purge data per policy. |
| **Issue Escalation** | Manage conflicts and ensure timely resolution. |
