# Platform Overview

The Tenx Platform is organized into three main platforms supported by a management dashboard. Together they cover the full talent lifecycle from application through training to career placement.

## How the Platforms Connect

![Tenx Platform Map](assets/images/tenx.png)

## Platform Summary

### Tenx Learning

The core training platform. It handles the end-to-end learning experience including content delivery, assignment submission, automated grading, rubric-based feedback, trainee performance tracking, team management, and portfolio generation.

**Key capabilities:**

- Assignment submission and automated grading
- Rubric generation and feedback delivery
- Trainee and team dashboards
- Leaderboards and performance tracking
- Profile generation from trainee progress
- Chatbot-assisted learning support

---

### Apply

The application and selection platform. It manages the full applicant journey from application submission through assessment and selection.

**Key capabilities:**

- Applicant profiles and application forms
- AI-powered question generation for assessments
- Quiz management and scoring
- Staff review workflows
- Application status tracking

---

### Leap

The career readiness and job matching platform. It supports trainees transitioning from learning into professional careers.

**Key capabilities:**

- Job browsing and intelligent job recommendations
- Professional profile building and competency extraction
- Interview preparation with AI HR persona (Parrot)
- Career scenario simulation and coaching
- CV analysis and enhancement
- Monitoring and notification services for engagement

---

### Management Dashboard

The administrative control center for program managers and operations staff. It provides oversight across all programs, cohorts, and platform services.

**Key capabilities:**

- Program and cohort management
- Service monitoring and operations
- Cross-platform reporting

The three main platforms communicate with each other through shared APIs and services to provide a seamless experience across the full user journey.
