# ***Tenx Content & Learning Design Manual***

## ***1\. Purpose***

*Provide **Content Designers, Instructors (Staff)** and **Super Admins** with a clear guide for creating, personalising, delivering, and evaluating learning materials on Tenx.*

---

## ***2\. Roles & Responsibilities***

| *Role* | *Responsibilities* |
| ----- | ----- |
| ***Super Admin*** | *\- Enable roadmap features (e.g., differential week assignments, large‑scale grading) \- Define global training‑goal standards \- Manage roles & permissions for content teams* |
| ***Staff – Content Designer / Instructor*** | *\- Create & manage challenge docs, quizzes, review forms \- Configure smart rubrics, peer‑grading, automated grading \- Personalise assignments and workloads \- Monitor dashboards, leaderboards, insights* |

---

## ***3\. Content Creation Workflow***

1. ***Draft Challenge / Module Document***  
    *• Use **Manage → Challenge Document** to upload or edit the master file.*

2. ***Generate Smart Rubric***  
    *• Run **Generate Smart Rubrics** to align criteria with learning goals.*

3. ***Set Submission Parameters***  
    *• Define deadlines, resubmission rules, peer‑grading windows.*

4. ***Link Review Forms & Quizzes***  
    *• Create / duplicate formative surveys, interviews, or review forms.*

5. ***Publish & Notify***  
    *• Schedule bulk email or in‑app notifications to the cohort.*

***Tip:** Duplicate proven forms to ensure consistency across cohorts.*

---

## ***4\. Personalisation Strategies (2024‑2025)***

| *Feature* | *Application* |
| ----- | ----- |
| *Training‑Goal Specification* | *Set competency targets per cohort → drive rubric weights* |
| *Challenge Document Personalisation* | *Swap content blocks or difficulty by learner group* |
| *Differential Week Assignments* | *Adjust task volume by cohort pace* |
| *Personalised Evaluation* | *Map rubric criteria to individual goals; leverage smart grading* |
| *Automated Submission Grading* | *Activate large‑scale grading for instant feedback* |

---

## ***5\. Nana‑Powered Support***

### ***5.1 For Staff***

* ***Cohort Manager Mode**: manage schedules, auto resource allocation, alerts*

* ***Tutor Mode**: maintain challenge‑doc knowledge base, enforce \< 1 min support latency, push content recommendations*

### ***5.2 For Trainees***

* ***Context Switch**: chat with Nana on personal, cohort, program, or job‑market context*

* ***Dynamic Charts & Dashboards**: visualise progress instantly*

***Reminder:** Schedule knowledge‑base refreshes to avoid context drift.*

---

## ***6\. Assessment & Feedback Cycle***

1. ***Submission Collection** – learners Submit / Edit work.*

2. ***Smart Grading** – automated rubric, manual override as needed.*

3. ***Peer Grading** – cross‑review reinforces criteria.*

4. ***Feedback Discussion** – learners discuss results; Staff moderate.*

5. ***Leaderboard & Dashboards** – weekly performance, engagement, competency radar.*

---

## ***7\. Monitoring Learning Effectiveness***

* *Review **weekly dashboards**: best submissions, engagement (Slack/GMeet), competency radar.*

* *Leverage **Eagle insights** (Engagement, Satisfaction, Experience) to flag at‑risk learners.*

* *Plan **automated follow‑ups** for low engagement or submissions (roadmap).*
