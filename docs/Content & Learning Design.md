# **_Tenx Content & Learning Design Manual_**

## **_1\. Purpose_**

_Provide **Content Designers, Instructors (Staff)** and **Super Admins** with a clear guide for creating, personalising, delivering, and evaluating learning materials on Tenx._

---

## **_2\. Roles & Responsibilities_**

| _Role_                                      | _Responsibilities_                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **_Super Admin_**                           | _\- Enable roadmap features (e.g., differential week assignments, large‑scale grading) \- Define global training‑goal standards \- Manage roles & permissions for content teams_                              |
| **_Staff – Content Designer / Instructor_** | _\- Create & manage challenge docs, quizzes, review forms \- Configure smart rubrics, peer‑grading, automated grading \- Personalise assignments and workloads \- Monitor dashboards, leaderboards, insights_ |

---

## **_3\. Content Creation Workflow_**

1. **_Draft Challenge / Module Document_**  
   _• Use **Manage → Challenge Document** to upload or edit the master file._

2. **_Generate Smart Rubric_**  
   _• Run **Generate Smart Rubrics** to align criteria with learning goals._

3. **_Set Submission Parameters_**  
   _• Define deadlines, resubmission rules, peer‑grading windows._

4. **_Link Review Forms & Quizzes_**  
   _• Create / duplicate formative surveys, interviews, or review forms._

5. **_Publish & Notify_**  
   _• Schedule bulk email or in‑app notifications to the cohort._

**\*Tip:** Duplicate proven forms to ensure consistency across cohorts.\*

---

## **_4\. Personalisation Strategies (2024‑2025)_**

| _Feature_                            | _Application_                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _Training‑Goal Specification_        | _Set competency targets per cohort → drive rubric weights_        |
| _Challenge Document Personalisation_ | _Swap content blocks or difficulty by learner group_              |
| _Differential Week Assignments_      | _Adjust task volume by cohort pace_                               |
| _Personalised Evaluation_            | _Map rubric criteria to individual goals; leverage smart grading_ |
| _Automated Submission Grading_       | _Activate large‑scale grading for instant feedback_               |

---

## **_5\. Nana‑Powered Support_**

### **_5.1 For Staff_**

- **\*Cohort Manager Mode**: manage schedules, auto resource allocation, alerts\*

- **\*Tutor Mode**: maintain challenge‑doc knowledge base, enforce \< 1 min support latency, push content recommendations\*

### **_5.2 For Trainees_**

- **\*Context Switch**: chat with Nana on personal, cohort, program, or job‑market context\*

- **\*Dynamic Charts & Dashboards**: visualise progress instantly\*

**\*Reminder:** Schedule knowledge‑base refreshes to avoid context drift.\*

---

## **_6\. Assessment & Feedback Cycle_**

1. **\*Submission Collection** – learners Submit / Edit work.\*

2. **\*Smart Grading** – automated rubric, manual override as needed.\*

3. **\*Peer Grading** – cross‑review reinforces criteria.\*

4. **\*Feedback Discussion** – learners discuss results; Staff moderate.\*

5. **\*Leaderboard & Dashboards** – weekly performance, engagement, competency radar.\*

---

## **_7\. Monitoring Learning Effectiveness_**

- _Review **weekly dashboards**: best submissions, engagement (Slack/GMeet), competency radar._

- _Leverage **Eagle insights** (Engagement, Satisfaction, Experience) to flag at‑risk learners._

- _Plan **automated follow‑ups** for low engagement or submissions (roadmap)._
