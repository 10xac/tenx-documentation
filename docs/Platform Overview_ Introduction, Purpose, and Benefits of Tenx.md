# Platform Overview Introduction, Purpose, and Benefits of Tenx

### **Introduction**

**Tenx** is an advanced ed-tech platform designed by 10 Academy to bridge the gap between educational training and real-world application. It is a comprehensive system that serves both administrative staff and trainees by providing tools that facilitate effective management of educational content, assessment, and communication.

#### **Overview of Tenx**

Tenx offers a robust framework for user management and data analytics. It supports a wide array of functionalities tailored to enhance the learning experience and streamline administrative tasks. The platform features an intuitive interface where users can manage multiple programs and cohorts, interact with a rich question bank, and utilize detailed analytics for real-time insights.

#### **Purpose of Tenx**

The primary purpose of Tenx is to equip trainees with practical skills and knowledge that directly translate to job market demands, while providing administrators with powerful tools to oversee progress and engagement. It simplifies the administration of complex training programs and increases the efficiency of learning delivery by automating many aspects of the educational process.

#### **Benefits of the System**

For trainees, Tenx provides a dynamic learning environment equipped with smart grading, and instant feedback mechanisms. It supports a wide variety of learning activities such as quizzes, assignments, and project submissions, all integrated with real-time performance dashboards.

For administrators, Tenx offers extensive capabilities in user and content management, including the creation and duplication of forms, monitoring submission statistics, and managing job placement activities. Its analytical tools enable administrators to make informed decisions based on comprehensive data insights, from individual performance to cohort-wide trends.
