# Glossary

Terminology used across the Tenx platform — pinned to a single canonical
meaning so the same word means the same thing wherever you read it.

> Don't see your term? See [FAQ](faq.md) or contact `support@10academy.org`.

---

## A

### Applicant
A candidate going through Apply — has registered, may have submitted an
application, and may still be in selection. After acceptance, an applicant
becomes a **Trainee** in Tenx Learning. See [Apply Applicant Guide](platforms/apply/applicant.md).

### Application package
The bundle configured in Apply that ties together an *applicant-facing form*,
an optional *reviewer form*, and an optional *pre-assessment*. Treated as one
unit for publishing, monitoring, and selection.

### Assets *(Apply)*
The Apply menu that holds reusable content — **Forms** and the **Question
Bank**. Anything created here can be reused across cohorts.

### Assessment
A timed, automatically-scored quiz delivered through Apply. Used as a
pre-assessment for selection or as a checkpoint within a cohort. *Assessments
are not the same as interviews* — interviews live in Leap.

### Assignment
A graded piece of work submitted on Tenx Learning. Carries a rubric, deadlines,
reviewers, and submission status. See [Assignment Management](platforms/tenx-learning/staff.md#328-assignment-management).

---

## B

### Batch
The Tenx Learning equivalent of a Cohort. The **Batch Selector** at the top of
the Home page is how staff switch between active groups of trainees.

### BCC (in bulk emails)
The hidden recipient field. Apply auto-populates BCC with the selected
applicants when you compose a bulk email so addresses stay private. Always use
BCC for mass mailing applicants.

---

## C

### Cohort
A specific intake within a Workspace — typically a numbered batch (Batch 1,
Batch 2…). One Workspace can contain many cohorts.

### Competency
A skill area tracked on the trainee profile, mapped to **SFIA levels**
(Skills Framework for the Information Age). Validated by staff or via project
work; feeds into Leap's job recommendation engine.

### Content type *(Tenx Learning)*
How a module delivers content — either **Section** (structured internal
content) or **Link** (external resource).

---

## D

### Draft *(application status)*
An application that has been started but not submitted. Visible in the
**Application Draft** tab so staff can identify drop-off and follow up.

---

## E

### Eagle
A Leap app. Monitors trainee engagement signals and sends notifications to
keep job seekers active in their search. See [Apps](apps.md#eagle-engagement-monitoring-notifications).

---

## F

### Form Type *(Apply)*
The category of a form built in Apply's Form Builder. Six types are supported:
Application, Survey, Evaluation, Review, **Interview**, and Analytics. The
*Interview* type is consumed by Leap, not Apply.

### Frog
A Leap app. The intelligent job-matching engine that recommends opportunities
based on the trainee's competencies, preferences, and goals. See [Apps](apps.md#frog-job-recommendation-engine).

---

## G

### Grade History
The audit trail on every Tenx Learning submission. Records who graded, when,
and what score. Allows reviewers to revert to a previous state with **Make
Current**. See [Grade History](platforms/tenx-learning/staff.md#3287-grade-history-audit-version-control).

### Group
An optional sub-division within a Cohort/Batch — used to assign different
content or reviewers to subsets of trainees.

---

## I

### Instructor
Public-facing term for staff who deliver training and evaluate work.
Includes **Program Coordinators**, **Reviewers**, and **Tutors**. The in-product
UI uses **Staff**. Treat them as interchangeable.

### Interview
The hiring-flow conversation between a candidate and an employer. **Interviews
live in Leap, not Apply.** Apply has an *Interview* form type, but it's just
the template Leap uses to capture interviewer responses.

### iPersona
The brand name for the **Parrot** app in the UI. CV analysis and competency
extraction. Same service.

---

## L

### Leap
The career readiness and job matching platform — the third stage of the Tenx
lifecycle (Apply → Tenx Learning → Leap). See [Leap Overview](platforms/leap/index.md).

### Learner
Public-facing term for **Trainee**. The role page uses *Learner*; the
in-product UI uses *Trainee*.

---

## M

### Management Dashboard
The administrative control center for organization staff. Manages programs,
cohorts, service oversight, and cross-platform reporting. See [Management Dashboard](platforms/dashboard/index.md).

### Manual Rubric
A rubric authored entirely by staff (no AI assistance). Used when you need
full control over scoring structure. Contrast with **Smart Rubric** and
**Rubrics with AI**.

### Module
A learning unit within a Topic on Tenx Learning. Contains sections,
attachments, and a list of assigned trainees.

---

## O

### Onboarding *(Tenx Learning)*
Introductory materials shown to trainees at the start of their journey.
Created and managed in **Content → Onboarding** by staff.

---

## P

### Parrot
A Leap app. The internal name for the AI CV-analysis service that's branded as
**iPersona** in the UI.

### Pre-assessment
An assessment that an applicant must complete before review can begin.
Attached to an Application package in Apply. See [Add Pre-assessment](platforms/apply/staff.md#step-3-add-pre-assessment).

### Program
A workspace in Apply (e.g., AI Engineering). Contains one or more cohorts.

### Program Coordinator
The staff role responsible for configuring cohorts, forms, assessments, and
reviewers in Apply. The most common type of *Instructor*.

---

## Q

### Question Bank
Apply's library of reusable assessment questions. Organised by Type (SQL,
Python, Maths, Statistics), Level (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced), and Tag
(Raw, Qualified, Unqualified, Mock). Only **Qualified** questions should reach
applicants. See [Question Bank](platforms/apply/staff.md#333-question-bank).

### Qualified *(question tag)*
A question that has been reviewed and approved for use in live assessments.
The recommended-only tag for production use.

---

## R

### Reviewer
A staff member who evaluates applicant responses against a rubric and submits
scores. See [Reviews](platforms/apply/staff.md#344-reviews).

### Rubric
The scoring criteria for an assignment or assessment. Three creation modes on
Tenx Learning: **Smart Rubric** (AI-assisted), **Rubrics with AI** (auto-generated),
and **Manual Rubric** (fully staff-defined).

---

## S

### Section
A unit of content within a module on Tenx Learning. Contains rich-text body,
optional references, and tags.

### SFIA Level
Skills Framework for the Information Age — the competency-rating scale used on
trainee profiles to indicate proficiency in each skill area.

### Smart Rubric
An AI-assisted rubric on Tenx Learning. Generates structure quickly; can be
edited after generation. Contrast with **Manual Rubric**.

### Staff
The in-product UI label for *Instructors* — Program Coordinators, Reviewers,
Tutors. Same role.

### Status *(application)*
Where the applicant is in the pipeline. Values: Draft, Submitted, Assessment
Pending, Assessment Complete, Under Review, Reviewed, Accepted, Waitlisted,
Rejected.

### Submission Window
The time interval between an assignment's Open Date and Closing Date during
which trainees may submit. Once closed, the portal rejects further submissions.

### Survey
A feedback form delivered through Apply at any cohort stage. Built using
**Form Type: Survey**.

---

## T

### Tenx Learning
The training platform. Stage two of the Tenx lifecycle. See [Tenx Learning Overview](platforms/tenx-learning/index.md).

### Topic
A subject area on Tenx Learning that organises related modules. Trainees are
assigned access to topics, which gates access to the modules within.

### Trainee
The in-product UI label for *Learner*. The user enrolled in a cohort on Tenx
Learning. Same role.

---

## W

### Workspace
A program-level container in Apply. Contains one or more **Cohorts**. Staff
choose a workspace and cohort after login before drilling into the dashboard.
