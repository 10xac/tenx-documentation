# Getting Started

Welcome to the Tenx Platform. This guide will help you understand how to navigate and use the platform depending on your role.

## For Trainees

As a trainee, you will primarily interact with:

1. **[Apply](platforms/apply/index.md)** — Submit your application for a new program, take assessments, and track your application status.
2. **[Tenx Learning](platforms/tenx-learning/index.md)** — Access your training materials, submit assignments, view grades, receive feedback, and track your progress through the program.
3. **[Leap](platforms/leap/index.md)** — Explore job opportunities, build your professional profile, prepare for interviews with the AI HR persona (Parrot), get career recommendations, and practice with career scenario simulations.

## For Staff and Administrators

Staff members have access to additional tools:

1. **[Apply](platforms/apply/index.md)** — Create application cycles, review applications, manage question banks, and oversee the selection process.
2. **[Tenx Learning](platforms/tenx-learning/index.md)** — Manage trainees, review submissions, configure rubrics, and monitor performance.
3. **[Leap](platforms/leap/index.md)** — Manage job listings, review trainee career profiles, manage interview preparation tools (Parrot), and track placement outcomes.
4. **[Management Dashboard](platforms/dashboard/index.md)** — Oversee programs, cohorts, services, and platform operations.

## Getting Access & Support

If this is your first time, request demo access by contacting **support@10academy.org**. Your program coordinator will provide you with login credentials once approved. For any help using the platform, reach out to your program coordinator or the support team at the same address.
