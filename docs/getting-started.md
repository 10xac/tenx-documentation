# Getting Started

Welcome to the Tenx Platform. This guide will help you understand how to navigate and use the platform depending on your role.

## For Trainees

As a trainee, you will primarily interact with:

1. **Apply** — Submit your application for a new program, take assessments, and track your application status.
 - [Apply User Guide](platforms/apply/index.md)
2. **Tenx Learning** — Access your training materials, submit assignments, view grades, receive feedback, and track your progress through the program.
 - [Tenx Learning User Guide](platforms/tenx-learning/index.md)
3. **Leap** — Explore job opportunities, build your professional profile, prepare for interviews with the AI HR persona (Parrot), get career recommendations, and practice with career scenario simulations.
 - [Leap User Guide](platforms/leap/index.md)

### Getting Access

If this is your first time, request demo access by contacting **support@10academy.org**. Your program coordinator will provide you with login credentials once approved.

## For Staff and Administrators

Staff members have access to additional tools:

- **Apply** — Create application cycles, review applications, manage question banks, and oversee the selection process
 - [Apply User Guide](platforms/apply/index.md)
- **Tenx Learning** — Manage trainees, review submissions, configure rubrics, and monitor performance
 - [Tenx Learning User Guide](platforms/tenx-learning/index.md)
- **Leap** — Manage job listings, review trainee career profiles, manage interview preparation tools (Parrot), and track placement outcomes
 - [Leap User Guide](platforms/leap/index.md)
- **Management Dashboard** — Oversee programs, cohorts, services, and platform operations
 - [Dashboard Guide](platforms/dashboard/index.md)

### Getting Access

If this is your first time, request demo access by contacting **support@10academy.org**.

## Support

If you need help with the platform, reach out to your program coordinator or the support team at **support@10academy.org**.
