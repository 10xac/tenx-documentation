AI-Assisted Assignments

Smarter feedback, faster growth.

Tenx integrates intelligent assistants to help trainees understand assignment objectives, align their submissions with grading rubrics, and improve the quality of their work through instant support.

 How the Assistant Helps

Each assignment page includes an AI Assistant tab that supports trainees with:

  • Task summaries
  • Clarified objectives
  • Rubric-based expectations
  • Actionable feedback to improve submissions before grading

 Built-in Rubrics for Self-Evaluation

The assistant understands the assignment-specific rubric, breaking it into components like:

  • Project Understanding
  • Critical Thinking
  • Result and Insights

Each rubric level (e.g., High, Moderate, Average, Non-existent) is described in detail, helping trainees self-score and benchmark their work.

 

 Real-Time Guidance

Trainees can ask:

  • “What are the main objectives for this task?”
  • “Can you summarize the week’s challenge?”
  • “Can you give examples of high-quality work?”

The assistant replies instantly with structured, context-aware responses drawn from the challenge documents and rubric expectations.
