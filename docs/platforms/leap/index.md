## What It Does

Leap connects trained talent with career opportunities. Trainees use it to build professional profiles, explore job listings, receive personalized job recommendations, and prepare for career transitions. Staff use it to manage job listings, track placement outcomes, and support trainee career journeys.

**Key capabilities:**

- Job browsing and intelligent job recommendations
- Professional profile building and competency extraction
- Interview preparation with AI HR persona
- Career scenario simulation and coaching
- CV analysis and enhancement
- Monitoring and notification services for engagement
