Tenx Application Portal

Tenx Application Portal Staff Side Features

Forms and Survey Management

Staff can create, edit, duplicate, reuse, and preview various forms, including surveys, interview forms, and review forms. This feature is essential for collecting and managing data efficiently.

Program and Cohort Management 

Administrators can manage multiple programs and cohorts seamlessly, and track enrollment and progress.

Snapshot: Program management dashboard.

Individual and Group Stats Viewing 
Administrators can view aggregated information on applicants and detailed group statistics, enhancing the ability to make data-driven decisions.

Snapshot: Chart and table showing sample data or stats.

Question Bank Management 

The ability to add, edit, review, and label questions in the question bank, along with viewing its usage stats, helps in maintaining a robust assessment tool.

Snapshot: The question bank interface.

Bulk Emails and Notifications 

Staff can send bulk emails and manage notifications, including viewing and downloading email status reports, which facilitates efficient communication across large groups.

Snapshot: Bulk email interface.

Downloadable Data
Staff have the capability to download table data, which can be crucial for offline analysis or reporting.

Tenx Learning Platform

Tenx Learning Platform Staff Side Features

Assignment Dashboard

Administrators can view and manage all ongoing and past assignments through the "Assignment" interface.

Tenx provides functionalities for admins to add new assignments or adjust existing ones based on the evolving needs of the trainees and the curriculum.

Snapshot: Assignment interface

Submission and Engagement Tracking

Administrators can oversee and manage trainee submissions through a clear view of submission statuses—submitted, missing, or late—and allows for direct grading and feedback.

Tools for monitoring submission stats and reviewing draft submissions help in keeping track of trainee performance and engagement levels.


Snapshot: Assignment submissions tracking

Smart Rubric and Peer Grading 

Advanced tools like smart rubrics for automated grading and peer grading systems are available to ensure a fair and efficient assessment process.
