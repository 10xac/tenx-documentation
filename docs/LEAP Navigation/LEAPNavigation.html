LEAP Navigation Document

Welcome to the LEAP platform, part of our tenX initiative designed to support your job search during the intensive training program. This document provides a comprehensive guide to navigating and utilizing LEAP effectively.

This is the LEAP Login Page


Upon logging in, you will see the LEAP loading screen. This screen contains helpful tips and information about LEAP and the supported job search process.



Landing Page

After logging in, you will be directed to the landing page. Here, you will find three main drawers in the top-right corner:

  1. All Jobs
  2. My Jobs
  3. Profile



All Jobs

The All Jobs drawer offers job recommendations based on your profile. You can:

  • Analyze job openings
  • Provide feedback
  • Engage with job postings
  • View match details for jobs with an 80%+ match status


Top Icons

  • Leaped Icon: Displays the number of credits used today out of 10. Each day, you can generate CVs for jobs with an 80%+ match using up to 10 credits.
  • Super Liked Icon: Shows the number of jobs you have super liked.
  • Liked Icon: Shows the number of jobs you have liked.

Job Description Section

In this section, you can view detailed information about a recommended job:

  • Purpose
  • Duties and Responsibilities
  • Relevant Competencies
  • Required/Preferred Qualifications
  • Job Competency Listing

Use the left and right arrows to navigate through job details.

        


Providing Feedback

Trainees can provide feedback on the job displayed. Options include:

  • Liking or disliking specific elements
  • Super liking a job
  • Providing detailed feedback about responsibilities, role, and required attributes

Note: Your feedback influences future job recommendations.

Reaction Options

  • Skip: Options include Not Interested, Not This But Fits, Not This and All Like This.
  • Like: Shows interest without a final decision.
  • Super Like: Indicates strong interest and triggers automatic generation of application materials if the match score is 80% or higher.


You can also Enter tags, words, phrases to job applications to help the system learn your preferences and update the next set of jobs displayed to you.



You have 3 reactions. If you do not wish to react, you can toggle the comment switch off (located next to the ‘what do you like about this job section).




Either if you give at least one or two feedback or you switch off the feedback toggle. Then the Skip, Like and Super like button will be available to be clicked.
                
This accepts the reaction of the trainee:

  • Skip: has three options : Not Interested, Not This But Fits, Not This and All Like This
  • Like: You're interested but haven't made a final decision yet.
  • Super Like; You're very interested and definitely want to apply for the job. If the match score is 80% or higher, application materials will be generated automatically.

    View details will take the trainee to an additional link to further pursue the Job post




Match Score

The match score indicates the percentage match between your profile and the job description. Click to view a detailed summary of the match score.




My Jobs

The My Jobs drawer contains jobs you have engaged with, divided into two tabs:

  • Engagement Tab: Lists all engagements with jobs.
  • Leaped Tab: Displays super liked jobs with high match status. You can download auto-generated CVs and cover letters and apply using the appropriate application links.



Engagement tab lists out all engagements of the trainee with a job

Leaped tab is where the Super liked Jobs with a high match status are displayed

You  can download CV, and Cover letter that is auto generated according to the profile and skills of the trainee that relate to the particular Job. You can also apply using the appropriate application links listed

Profile

The Profile drawer allows you to manage your profile and skills.

You have two tabs above, your Profile details, and your skills.

On the top right corner, you can find your general links and personal contact

Bio and Work Experience: Add and update your work experience.

Education: Add and update your educational background.


Projects: Add projects you've worked on during or outside the intensive training.


Files and Content: Upload files for review and addition to your profile.

Skills Tab

This tab displays your current skills and skills you can or should acquire. You can request to upgrade skills after demonstrating proficiency.
