# Account Management

Effective account management is the foundation for getting the most out of Tenx. This page walks you through everything you need—from creating your account for the first time to completing your profile so the platform can serve you the best-matched content and opportunities.

---

### **1\. Creating Your Account**

#### **1\. Prerequisites**

- A valid email address you can access.
- (If required) A referral code or cohort registration link provided by 10 Academy.

#### **Step-by-Step**

1. **Choose Your Language**

   - _From the Tenx landing page, select your preferred language in the upper-right corner._

2. **Login (or Sign-Up)**

   - _Open the Sign-Up page._

   - _Click Sign Up / Create Account._

3. **Choose your sign-up method**

   - _Email & Password_ (default)

   - _Single Sign-On (SSO)_ with Google or GitHub (if your program enables it).

4. **Complete the registration form**

   - **Full Name** – Use your real, legal name (appears on certificates).

   - **Email** – This becomes your login username.

   - **Password** – Minimum 8 characters, with at least one number and one symbol.

   - **Program / Cohort** – Select the cohort you’re joining (e.g., “U2J Cohort 2”).

   - **Referral Code** – Optional; leave blank if none.

5. **Accept the Terms**

   - Read the _Tenx Terms of Service_ and _Privacy Policy_.

   - Tick **I agree** to continue.

6. **Verify your email**

   - Tenx sends a one-time link to the address you entered.

   - Click **Confirm Email** in that message—your account is now active\!

7. **First-time login**

   - You’ll be directed to set up your profile (see **Profile Setup** below).

**Troubleshooting**  
 • No email after a few minutes? Check spam/junk folders.  
 • Link expired? Re-enter your email on the login page and click **Resend Verification**.

---

### **2\. Logging In**

#### **Standard Login**

1. Go to **app.tenx.10academy.org** (or your program’s custom subdomain).

2. Click **Log In**.

3. Enter the **email** and **password** you registered with.

4. Click **Continue**.

#### **Single Sign-On (if enabled)**

1. Select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**.

2. Authorize Tenx to use your account details.

3. You’re in—no separate password needed.

#### **Forgot Your Password?**

1. On the login screen, click **Forgot Password?**

2. Enter your registered email and submit.

3. Check your email for a reset link (valid for 30 minutes).

4. Create a new password, then log in as usual.

**Security Tips**  
 • Never share your password or verification codes.  
 • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) under **Settings → Security** for extra protection.

### **3\. Profile Setup**

Building a complete profile helps Tenx tailor job matches, learning paths, and analytics to you.

| Section                   | What to Fill In                                              | Why It Matters                                                    |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Personal Details**      | Name, preferred display name, phone, location (city/country) | Appears on certificates and helps recruiters filter local talent. |
| **Professional Summary**  | Short bio (1–2 sentences)                                    | Gives context to mentors and hiring partners.                     |
| **Skills & Competencies** | Add languages, frameworks, and tools you’re confident in.    | Powers the Competency Matching algorithm for jobs and challenges. |
| **Education**             | Degree, institution, graduation year                         | Validates your academic background.                               |
| **Experience**            | Roles, companies, dates, responsibilities                    | Improves job-fit scoring.                                         |
| **Portfolio Links**       | GitHub, LinkedIn, personal website                           | Lets reviewers see your work directly.                            |
| **Profile Picture**       | High-resolution headshot (300 × 300 px)                      | Builds trust and personal connection.                             |
| **Notifications**         | Email/push preferences                                       | Control how Tenx contacts you.                                    |

#### **Saving & Editing**

- Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of each section before navigating away.

- You can update your profile anytime under **My Account → Profile**.

**Tip**  
 A profile completion bar shows your progress. Aim for 100 % to unlock personalized recommendations and visibility on the **Trainee Leaderboard**.
